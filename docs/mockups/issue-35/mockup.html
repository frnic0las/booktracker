BookTracker — app icon

Issue #35 · An open book seen head-on — two pages splaying up and away from a centre gutter — with an accent bookmark ribbon rising out of the fold and hanging below it. The ribbon is the whole idea: a book alone says library, a book with a bookmark says I am partway through this — which is what BookTracker tracks.

Every board below renders the same SVG source (see the last board). Nothing here is a bitmap.

1 · The mark

Left: the asset as exported — a square PNG, corners untouched. Right: how iOS actually draws it, masked into the home-screen squircle. Never bake the rounded corners into the file.

Why a dark tile and not an accent-blue one? The app is a dark-first, iOS-native surface — #0a0a0b everywhere. A blue tile would be the only place the accent behaves as a background rather than an action colour, and it would collide with the dozens of blue system icons already on the home screen. The dark field with a faint accent bloom keeps the blue meaning exactly what it means inside the app: this is the thing you touch.

2 · Size ladder

The mark holds down to 29px because it is three shapes, not thirty: two pages and a ribbon. There are no page lines, no serifs, no text — the first details to die under a 3× downscale.

3 · On a home screen

The real test: does a near-black tile survive next to native apps, on a dark wallpaper, on a light one, and on a loud one? The neighbours below are flat colour stubs — this repo ships no third-party logos.

On a dark wallpaper a dark tile risks dissolving. It does not here, because the two pages are near-white #ffffff → #cfcfd6 and occupy 62% of the canvas width — the tile's edge may blur into the wallpaper, but the mark never does. That is the same trade every dark iOS icon makes.

4 · Maskable variant

Android crops the icon to a shape the OEM chooses. Everything outside the inner 80% may be cut. The red circle is the guaranteed-visible zone; the green square is the 80% box the spec draws it inside.

80% safe circle 80% safe square
Deviation from the issue. The brief suggests the maskable background be "typically the accent colour". It should not be — the standard tile's background is already a solid field that runs to all four edges, so the maskable variant needs no new background. Swapping in accent blue would ship two different-looking icons for the same app depending on the platform. The only difference between icon-512.png and icon-maskable-512.png is the glyph scale: 100% → 90%. At 90% no point of the glyph sits more than 170px from the centre, inside the 204.8px safe radius.

5 · Legibility stress tests

Grayscale checks the mark does not depend on the blue. Blur checks the silhouette at a glance. Threshold checks it survives being crushed to two tones.

6 · Splash screens

Splash is the glyph alone on a solid field — no tile, no rounded corners, no wordmark. It appears for a few hundred milliseconds before the app shell paints, so it must be the same colour as the shell or the launch flashes. The glyph sits at 45% of the short edge, nudged 4% above centre so it reads as optically centred.

The light splash inverts the glyph — pages go to #1c1c1e and the ribbon to #007aff, exactly the light-mode values of --primary and --accent in globals.css. White pages on a white field would be an empty screen with a floating blue ribbon.

7 · Colour

Every value is already in globals.css. The gradients are shading of existing tokens, not new colours.

Element Value Token
Tile field, top #17171c lift of --background
Tile field, bottom #0a0a0b --background
Accent bloom #0a84ff @ 22% → 0% --accent
Pages, top #ffffff lift of --primary
Pages, bottom #cfcfd6 shade of --primary
Ribbon, top #4ba7ff lift of --accent
Ribbon, bottom #0a6fe0 ≈ --accent-pressed
Splash, light — pages #1c1c1e --primary (light)
Splash, light — ribbon #007aff --accent (light)

8 · SVG source

Canvas is 512×512. The glyph's bounding box is 316×222 centred at (256, 256) — 62% of the canvas width, inside Apple's optical grid. Shipped verbatim as icon.svg; icon-maskable.svg is the same file with the glyph group wrapped in scale(0.9) about the centre.
